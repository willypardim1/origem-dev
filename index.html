FAQ

Questions, answered

What does "unlimited" mean?

Submit as many requests as you like. We work through them in order, one (or more, depending on your plan) at a time.

Is the website really free?

Yes, on the Unlimited Graphics ($490/mo) and Pro ($590/mo) plans. Those churches get a custom website designed and built at no extra charge. If you already have one, we update and fix it instead.

Who owns the designs?

You do. All final files are yours to use however your ministry needs.

What happens to my website if I cancel?

We'll talk through options with you — including handing the site over so you keep it.

Does the Social Media plan include a website?

No. The free website (or free updates to your existing site) comes with Unlimited Graphics and Pro. You can upgrade anytime.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts.

What file formats do I get?

PNG, JPG, PDF and source files on request, sized for screens, print and social.
